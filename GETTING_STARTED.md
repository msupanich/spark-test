# Getting the Phantasy Star ROM

## How to legally obtain the ROM

Since Phantasy Star is copyrighted software, you must own the original game to use this ROM.

### Option 1: Create a Backup (Recommended)
If you own the original Sega Master System cartridge:

1. **Using a Flash Cartridge**
   - Purchase a flash cartridge (like Rumble Station or EverDrive)
   - Connect it to your computer
   - Copy the ROM from the flash cartridge's menu

2. **Using a ROM Dumper**
   - Connect your cartridge to a ROM dumper
   - Extract the ROM file to your computer

### Option 2: Check Availability
Phantasy Star has been re-released on:
- **Sega Mega Drive/Genesis** (as part of collections)
- **Sega Saturn** (in Sega Ages)
- **PlayStation** (Sega Ages)
- **Modern consoles** (Sega Mega Drive Classic, etc.)

Purchase a digital version and extract the ROM if the platform allows it.

## What file do I need?

For this emulator, you need:
- **File name**: `Phantasy Star (World).sms` or `Phantasy Star (World).bin`
- **Size**: Usually 512KB (4Mb) for the World version
- **Format**: Sega Master System ROM (.sms or .bin)

## Adding the ROM to the Emulator

1. Open the GitHub Pages site
2. Click "Select ROM File"
3. Navigate to and select your Phantasy Star ROM
4. Press "Start Game"
